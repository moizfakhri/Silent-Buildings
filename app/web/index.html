Read this first

This map shows model estimates for research, not findings about any owner or tenant. Colours are predictions made from public data, and they are wrong for many individual buildings.

  • Predicted risk: how likely the building is to see a vacate order, emergency repair charge, AEP selection or HPD litigation in the next 12 months.
  • Predicted condition: the same idea using only age, size and earlier city actions, with no complaint information.
  • Complaint gap: predicted condition compared with the building's complaint record. A large gap does not mean a building is hiding danger. In the validation behind this map, buildings that are quieter than predicted had slightly fewer later enforcement events than predicted, not more.
  • No-access share: how many closed complaints ended with the inspector unable to gain access. It is real and sizeable, but buildings with a high share did not show extra later risk.

Most buildings (about 77%) have no HPD complaint in a year, so silence is ordinary. Do not use this map to judge a building, a landlord or a neighbourhood. Method and limits: docs/silent_distress_phase4.md and docs/modeling_phase3.md.
